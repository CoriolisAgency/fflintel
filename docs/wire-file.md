**Last public monthly on Outdoor Wire as of this desk (13 Aug 2026):** the 13 Apr 2026 March file. Do not invent April–July 2026 prints.

**Their method (paraphrase only from the Index files):** compile POS and ecommerce from the network; isolate new firearm sales; same-store (retailers reporting in both the current and prior-year window); report variance as a percent. Aimed at distortions from openings/closings and from NICS (permits, used guns, transfers, denials, multi-gun checks). “Figures are subject to revision as retailer data syncs are finalized.” About blurb: “transaction data from over 2,000 U.S. firearms retailers.” June 2024 Q1 report said 2,500 contributing retailers — use the About line on the Index files (2,000+), do not litigate the delta.

**NICS-flaw argument (their words, 2024–2026):** NICS is an imprecise proxy for new-gun demand. Outdoor Wire, 6 Jun 2024 (Kaleb Seymour, VP Data Analytics) and restated on every Index release.

**Named quotes (theirs, not ours):**

- Seymour, 6 Jun 2024: gap between background-check volume and firearms sold; “multiple purchases per check or denials, which the NICS reports do not address.”
- Seymour, 3 Sep 2025 launch: “a transparent, data-driven benchmark that reflects actual new consumer purchases at the retail counter.”
- Seymour, 29 Jan 2026 (Tactical Wire annual): “In this market, narrative isn't enough — the difference is visibility.”

## Annual / outlook / category color

- Tactical Wire, 29 Jan 2026: 2025 same-store new firearm unit sales down about **13%** vs 2024; inventory declined YoY; handguns comparatively resilient; rifles and shotguns deeper declines; some segments showing price competition.
- NSSF, 23 Feb 2026 (Gearfire complimentary 2025 Annual / 2026 forecast):
  - 2025 same-store new firearm units ~**−13%** vs 2024; Gearfire 2026 outlook another year of decline, projected **7–12%**.
  - New retail firearms 2025: shotguns **−16%**, rifles **−15.4%**, handguns **−11.5%** (still highest-volume category). Vs 2019 baseline: **−10.5%** new firearms.
  - Year-end firearm inventory **−10.6%** YoY, ~**3%** below 2019.
  - Ammo: units **−12.4%**, revenue **−16.7%**, ASP **−4.8%**; vs 2019 ammo units **+2.6%**.
  - Optics: units **−14.7%**, revenue **−14.8%**; vs 2019 combined optics **+38.2%**; red dots **+121%** vs 2019.
  - Suppressors: units **−19.3%**, revenue **−19.6%** after a strong 2024; Gearfire attributes some of the drop to buyers waiting on the $200 tax-stamp change; suppressor inventory **+21.8%** YoY; ASP **−0.5%**.
