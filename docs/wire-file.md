**Their method (paraphrase only from the 13 Apr 2026 file):** compile POS and ecommerce from the network; isolate new firearm sales; same-store (retailers reporting in both the current and prior-year window); report variance as a percent. Aimed at distortions from openings/closings and from NICS (permits, used guns, transfers, denials, multi-gun checks). “Figures are subject to revision as retailer data syncs are finalized.” About blurb on that file: “transaction data from over 2,000 U.S. firearms retailers.”

**NICS-flaw argument (their words, 2024–2026):** NICS is an imprecise proxy for new-gun demand. Outdoor Wire / Gearfire blog, 6 Jun 2024 and restated on every Index release.
