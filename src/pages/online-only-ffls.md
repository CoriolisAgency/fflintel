ATF's public listing of federal firearms licensees tells you how many licenses were on a named month's file, and of what type. It can't tell you how many of those licensees sell only online or how many work from a home, because those aren't fields on the official file. A count of "home-based FFLs" or "online-only FFLs" on a third-party map is that author's label, not ATF's, and we will not invent one.

## What the official file contains

The listing is a license file, and each row is a license.

The July 2026 complete list we counted for [how many FFLs are there](/how-many-ffls/) has these columns: license region, district and county codes, license type, an expiration code, a sequence number, business name, premises street, city, state and ZIP, mailing street, city, state and ZIP, and a voice phone number, plus one more column with a blank header.

That's the whole file: there's no column for how a licensee sells, and none for what kind of building the premises is.

For the citable count, go to [how many FFLs are there](/how-many-ffls/), which has 77,485 licensees, counted from ATF's July 2026 file, excluding Type 03.

## What the file does not contain

The file has no "online only" flag, no "home based" flag and no "sold a gun this month" flag.

Some sites run licensee addresses through a geocoder and call some of them residential. That's their classification rather than an ATF category, and this desk won't treat it as one or reprint those shares.

A Type 01 dealer license at an address that looks like a house still tells you nothing about how that dealer sells, and the file doesn't say.

## Why people ask anyway

People ask how many home-based FFLs there are, and it's a fair question, but ATF doesn't publish a count. A number built from address guesses is the estimate of whoever built it, not an ATF figure.

## If you don't have a storefront

A dealer without a counter still has to source inventory through channels the law allows, and still has to be found by shoppers.

## Short answers

**How many home-based FFLs are there?** There's no official count, because ATF's public listing has no field that flags a licensee as home-based, and we won't build one from address guesses. The official figures are license counts by type and state, from a named month's ATF file; see [how many FFLs are there](/how-many-ffls/).

**How many online-only FFLs are there?** There's no official count; the same file has no field that flags a licensee as online-only, and a Type 01 license at a given address isn't a statement about how that licensee sells.

**What can the official file tell you?** For a named month, it tells you how many licenses were listed, of what type, and the information ATF publishes for each one. It can't tell you who made a sale that month, who sells only online, or who works from a home.

## How we update

If ATF ever adds a field that answers this question, this page changes; until then the answer stays "the file can't tell you." The countable series live on [how many FFLs are there](/how-many-ffls/), and if that page moves to a newer ATF month, this one gets checked too; that's [how we cite](/methodology/).
